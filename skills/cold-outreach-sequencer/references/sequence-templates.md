# Cold Outreach Sequence Templates

Five-touch sequence templates with `{{variable}}` placeholders.
Channel annotations: [EMAIL], [LINKEDIN], [TWITTER].

---

## Touch 1 — Day 0: Introduction

**[EMAIL]**

> Subject A: "[Specific pain point] — quick question"
> Subject B: "{{recent_trigger}} → thought of you"
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> [One sentence about their context — reference {{recent_trigger}} or {{company}} detail.]
>
> I'm {{sender_name}}, {{sender_hook}}. We help {{icp_description}} to
> {{value_proposition}}.
>
> [One concrete question about their current process — not a pitch.]
>
> Worth a quick conversation?
>
> [Signature]

**[LINKEDIN]**

> Hi {{first_name}} — [One sentence acknowledging their context].
>
> [One question about their current approach to {{pain_point}}.]
>
> Happy to share what we've seen work. Open to connecting?

**[TWITTER]**

> Hey {{first_name}}, saw your thread on [topic]. Quick Q: how do you
> currently handle {{pain_point}}? Building something in this space and
> curious about your workflow.

## Touch 2 — Day 3: Value-Add

**[EMAIL]**

> Subject A: "For EMs dealing with {{pain_point}}"
> Subject B: "{{data_point}} — relevant to your team?"
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Following up from Monday. Thought this might be useful:
>
> [Share a relevant data point, benchmark, or short framework. Not a pitch.]
>
> {{data_point}}
>
> We've seen this show up consistently with {{icp_description}} — particularly
> when {{pain_point}} is manual.
>
> Does this match what you're seeing?
>
> [Signature]

**[LINKEDIN]**

> {{first_name}} — sharing this in case it's useful: [one-line insight or
> resource about {{pain_point}}]. Curious if this matches your experience.

## Touch 3 — Day 7: Social Proof

**[EMAIL]**

> Subject A: "How {{similar_company}} handled {{pain_point}}"
> Subject B: "Quick update on what worked"
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> {{similar_company}} was dealing with the same challenge. Here's what they did:
>
> {{case_study}} [2–3 sentences: situation, what they changed, result.]
>
> {{mutual_connection}} connected us, actually — [name-drop if applicable].
>
> Would something like this be useful for {{company}}?
>
> [Signature]

**[LINKEDIN]**

> {{first_name}}, a team similar to yours {{case_study_one_line}}.
> Happy to share the details if relevant.

---

## Touch 4 — Day 14: Break-Up

**[EMAIL] [LINKEDIN]**

> Subject: "Should I stop reaching out?"
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> I've reached out a few times without hearing back — completely understand
> if the timing is off or this isn't a priority right now.
>
> If {{pain_point}} becomes something you're looking to solve, I'm happy
> to reconnect. Otherwise, I won't keep filling your inbox.
>
> Worth a conversation, or should I close the loop here?
>
> [Signature]

---

## Touch 5 — Day 30: Long-Tail Re-Engage

**[EMAIL ONLY]**

> Subject: "Thought of {{company}} when I saw this"
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> I saw {{trigger_event}} — [one sentence on why this is relevant to them].
>
> Made me think our earlier conversation might be more timely now. Still
> happy to share what we've learned with others in your position.
>
> Open to reconnecting?
>
> [Signature]

**Note:** Do not send a Day 30 touch on LinkedIn or Twitter/X. The channel
norms don't support that cadence; a late follow-up there will read as spam.
