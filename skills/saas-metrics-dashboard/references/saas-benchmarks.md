# SaaS Benchmark Table by ARR Stage

Sources: Bessemer Venture Partners State of the Cloud (2023-2024), OpenView
SaaS Benchmarks Report (2024), SaaStr Annual Benchmark Data (2024).

Note: These are median benchmarks for venture-backed B2B SaaS companies.
Bootstrapped or product-led growth companies may have different targets.
Use as a reference point, not an absolute standard.

---

## Pre-PMF (< $100k ARR)

At this stage, metrics are less meaningful than qualitative signals. Focus on
PMF validation (Sean Ellis 40% threshold), not metric optimization.

| Metric | Target / Good |
|---|---|
| MRR growth rate | > 20% month-over-month |
| Gross revenue churn | < 5% monthly |
| NRR | > 90% |
| LTV:CAC | Not yet meaningful (sample too small) |
| Quick Ratio | > 4 (easy new revenue > easy churn) |

## Metric Definitions

**Quick Ratio**: (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / Churned MRR
- Measures how efficiently you grow relative to how much you lose.
- QR < 1: you are shrinking. QR 1-2: grinding. QR > 4: strong growth engine.

**Magic Number**: Net new ARR / Sales & Marketing spend in prior quarter
- Measures efficiency of growth spend. < 0.5 = inefficient. > 1 = very efficient.
- Use quarterly data (not monthly) for this calculation.

**Rule of 40**: ARR growth rate (%) + EBITDA margin (%)
- Balances growth and profitability. Score ≥ 40 is the public-market benchmark.
- Early stage: prioritize growth over margin. Post-$10M: both matter.

**NRR (Net Revenue Retention)**: (Starting MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Starting MRR
- > 100%: existing customers grow revenue even without new sales.
- The most important long-term indicator of product-market fit and land-and-expand
  motion health.

**LTV (simplified)**: ARPU / Gross Monthly Churn Rate
- Assumes constant churn rate (rarely true in practice; use as approximation).
- More accurate models discount future cash flows, but this approximation is
  sufficient for early-stage diagnosis.
