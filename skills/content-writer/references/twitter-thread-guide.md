# Twitter Thread Guide

Structural reference for the `content-writer` Twitter thread mode.

## Hook Formulas (Tweet 1)

A good hook does one of three things:

1. **Bold claim**: "Most [audience] are doing [thing] wrong."
2. **Curiosity gap**: "I spent [time/effort] learning [topic]. Here's what I found."
3. **Promise of value**: "[N] things [audience] wish they knew before [situation]. A thread."

**Anti-patterns:**
- Starting with "I": "I want to talk about..." — weak, self-centered.
- Starting with context: "As the founder of..." — no one cares yet.
- Rhetorical question as hook: "Ever wonder why...?" — overused, low signal.

## Body Tweets (Tweets 2–N)

Rules:
- One idea per tweet. Never split a sentence across two tweets.
- Each tweet should stand alone if quoted out of context.
- Use specific numbers: "40% of revenue" beats "a lot of revenue."
- Use examples: name a real pattern (not a real company without permission).
- Avoid filler: "This is so important" adds no information.

Tweet patterns that work:
- **Contrast**: "Most people do X. The best ones do Y instead."
- **Revelation**: "Here's the non-obvious reason why..."
- **Step**: "Step 1: [action]. Here's why this matters: [reason]."
- **Example**: "[Scenario]. What they found: [result]."

## Final Tweet (CTA)

Structure:
- Summarize the thread's core point in 1–2 sentences.
- One action: follow, reply, DM, click link.
- Optional: tag a relevant person or ask a question to drive replies.

Example:
> The pricing mistake isn't the number — it's the value metric. Find yours,
> test a 30% increase, and see what happens to conversion.
> 
> If this was useful, follow for more on founder-mode pricing. I post one
> framework per week.

## Thread Length

- 8 tweets: minimum for a meaningful thread.
- 12 tweets: optimal for most topics.
- 15 tweets: maximum. Beyond this, reader attention drops sharply.
- Avoid padding to hit a number. 9 tight tweets beat 15 loose ones.

## Formatting

- Use line breaks inside tweets for readability.
- Number the tweets if the sequence matters: "1/ This is the hook."
- Avoid excessive emoji. One per tweet maximum; zero is fine.
- No hashtags in body tweets (reduces readability). One hashtag in the
  final tweet is acceptable for discoverability.
