# ISO 27001 — Applicability and Checklist

## Five-Question Applicability Check

Answer Yes/No to each question:

1. Do your enterprise customers or prospects require ISO 27001 certification as a
   vendor qualification?
2. Do you operate in a regulated industry where ISO 27001 is a common baseline
   (finance, healthcare, government contracting)?
3. Do you handle confidential or proprietary information on behalf of customers?
4. Are you expanding into markets where ISO 27001 is more commonly required than
   SOC 2 (EU, Middle East, Asia-Pacific)?
5. Do you want a framework for building an Information Security Management System
   (ISMS) regardless of certification?

**Verdict**: If you answered Yes to ANY of the above, ISO 27001 is relevant.

---

## Scope Summary

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing,
maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System
(ISMS). Unlike SOC 2 (US-focused), ISO 27001 is internationally recognized.

The standard has 11 clauses (4-10 are mandatory) and Annex A with 93 controls
across four themes: Organizational, People, Physical, and Technological.

Certification requires a Stage 1 audit (documentation review) and Stage 2 audit
(operational effectiveness), plus annual surveillance audits and a 3-year
recertification cycle.

## Common Gaps for Early-Stage Companies

- No Statement of Applicability (SoA) — a mandatory document for certification.
- Risk register exists but controls are not linked to risks.
- No asset inventory (critical: you cannot protect what you haven't inventoried).
- No internal audit process or qualified internal auditor.
- Business continuity plan written but never tested.

---

## Remediation Priority Order

**P1** (required before Stage 1 audit):
- ISO-4.1: ISMS scope
- ISO-5.1: Information security policy
- ISO-5.3/5.4: Risk assessment and risk register
- ISO-5.5: Statement of Applicability
- ISO-6.2: Asset inventory

**P2** (required for Stage 2 audit — operating effectiveness):
- ISO-6.1: Access control policy and reviews
- ISO-6.4: SDLC policy
- ISO-6.6: Incident management
- ISO-6.9: Security awareness training
- ISO-6.10: Internal audit

**P3** (surveillance and continuous improvement):
- ISO-6.7: BCP testing
- ISO-6.8: Supplier security
- ISO-6.11: Annual management review
